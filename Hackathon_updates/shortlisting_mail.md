## Round 2 evaluation criteria

The review panel will assess:

1. Strength and relevance of the **problem statement**
2. Feasibility and innovation of the **solution**
3. Progress on **technical implementation**
4. Quality of **presentation** and supporting materials
5. Overall **readiness** for the final event

> **Panel guidance:** Working prototypes with completed features score higher than long future-feature lists. Focus on what can be realistically built and demonstrated within the timeframe.

## Organizer guidance for refining your solution

- **Narrow the problem** — avoid solving an entire sector or ecosystem
- **Name your audience** — who specifically benefits?
- **Pick one use case** — especially for public information / verification projects
- **Prioritize demo-ready features** — cut scope that cannot ship by June 13
- **Prototype > roadmap** — judges reward working software over wish lists
- **Stay niche** — general civic info systems should be focused and specific
- **Align naming** — team name and idea name should match what you build
- **One team only** — finalize participation with a single team if registered elsewhere
